# Interview Guide: Alumni or Neighborhood Event Group

Goal: learn how a small group closes event-related commitments without turning Quorum into volunteer scheduling or a general project manager. Duration: 20 minutes.

## Opening and consent

1. May I take anonymous notes about the workflow?
2. Please exclude attendee names, addresses, phone numbers, payment details, and access codes.
3. I will not request chat exports during this conversation. Is that acceptable?

## Current workflow

1. Reconstruct the last event from first proposal to final follow-up.
2. Which commitments were made in chat, and which had a separate tracking system?
3. Where did ownership become ambiguous?
4. Which decision generated the most messages relative to its importance?
5. Which action waited for consensus even though one or two accountable people could have decided?

## Authority and interruption

6. For venue holds, announcements, forms, and calendar changes, who has authority?
7. Which actions spend money or affect people outside the group?
8. Which actions can be undone, and for how long?
9. What default should apply after a 24-hour silence?
10. Who should be protected from repeated private requests?

## Evidence and outcome

11. What marks a commitment as completed?
12. How long do routine decisions usually take?
13. How many direct reminders feel excessive in one week?
14. What must appear in a weekly summary, and what must never appear?
15. Who can authorize a narrowly scoped, locally redacted one-week export and approve any public quotation?

## Close

Confirm the smallest plausible pilot scope. Do not promise integrations or results. Send the data-use scope and deletion date for separate approval.
