# Interview Guide: Community Book Club

Goal: identify coordination work around decisions, commitments, and follow-through without building another event-planning application. Duration: 20 minutes.

## Opening and consent

1. May I take anonymous structured notes?
2. Please avoid sharing names, contact details, or private messages.
3. The goal is fewer coordination interruptions, not increased participation pressure. Does that framing fit the group?

## Current workflow

1. Walk me through how the last reading choice became final.
2. Where did promises such as booking a room, bringing materials, or posting notes appear?
3. Which promises were lost in conversation, and how were they rediscovered?
4. Which decisions took longest, and what were people waiting for?
5. What routine actions could safely happen after a stated deadline without another poll?

## Authority and interruption

6. For book choice, venue, spending, and cancellation, who is the smallest sufficient decision group?
7. What is the acceptable default when nobody responds?
8. Which decisions should never be made from silence?
9. Would a maximum of two private decision requests per person per week feel acceptable? Why or why not?
10. What should a group receipt say so it is useful without restarting discussion?

## Evidence and outcome

11. How do you currently tell that a decision is closed?
12. Which four numbers would prove that coordination improved?
13. What would make you immediately remove the agent?
14. Could a synthetic replay be reviewed before any real export is considered?
15. Who could approve a one-week, locally redacted pilot and one exact public quotation?

## Close

Read back the candidate workflow and ask for corrections. Separate interest in a pilot from consent to access data; they are different approvals.
