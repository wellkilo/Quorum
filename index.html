Commitment Ledger

02

Autonomy Ladder

Repeated approvals earn autonomy. A rejection or undo lowers it immediately.

03

Minimum Quorum

Ask only the smallest safe set of accountable people, with a visible default.

04

Interrupt Budget

Two decision requests per person per rolling week. When spent, defer or reroute.
